## What this document is

a16z crypto's annual flagship report, **"State of Crypto 2025"** (publisher AH Capital Management; no individual bylines) (p.1-2). Its framing thesis: crypto has matured from a speculative, cyclical market into mainstream financial infrastructure — 2025 is "the year of institutional adoption," stablecoins have gone mainstream and rival the largest payment networks, the world is coming onchain (RWAs, DeFi, prediction markets), infrastructure is "(almost) ready for prime time," AI and crypto are converging, and the U.S. regulatory environment is the strongest the industry has seen. The report is organized into nine sections (p.3). This reference covers the market/adoption/stablecoin/policy themes; the throughput and chain-breakdown charts live in the companion chains-and-infrastructure reference.

## Market size and users

Bitcoin remains **>50% of total crypto market cap** (p.6). At the top of the asset-by-market-cap table (9/30/2025): Gold ~$26T, NVIDIA ~$4.5T, then Microsoft/Apple ~$3.8T, with **Bitcoin ~$2.3T** — "Gold is 11x bitcoin" (p.7). BTC's 2025 correlations: **~0.06 to Gold, ~0.47 to the Nasdaq** (p.8). Adoption: **716M global crypto owners** (Crypto.com, 8/31/2025), **181M monthly active addresses** (Allium, 9/30/2025), an estimated **40-70M monthly active users**, and ~10M users added in the last year (p.11). Developers peaked at ~33-34K/month and sit at ~25-32K; mobile-wallet users peaked ~45M in 2025 (p.5).

## Institutional adoption (2025 theme)

Bitcoin + Ethereum ETFs hold **>$175B onchain — $150B Bitcoin, $28B Ethereum** (p.14); BlackRock's IBIT was the fastest ETF ever to $10B/$25B/$50B AUM (p.14). Crypto treasury companies hold meaningful supply shares (BTC ~3.5%, ETH ~3.6%, SOL ~2.3% by Sept 2025), led by **MicroStrategy ($73B BTC)**, **BitMine/BMNR ($11B ETH)**, and **Forward/FORD ($1.4B SOL)** (p.15). Stablecoin mentions in SEC filings peaked ~315 in 2025 (p.16); **Circle IPO'd June 2025 at a >$50B market cap** (p.13).

## Stablecoins gone mainstream

Stablecoin transaction volume over the trailing 12 months reached **$46T unadjusted / $9T adjusted**, between PayPal ($1.7T) and ACH ($87T), with Visa at $16T (p.18). Cost/speed: a stablecoin transfer settles in **<1 second for <$0.01** (on Solana and Ethereum L2s), versus 2-3%+$0.30 for credit cards and $30-50 for international wires (p.19). Stablecoins collectively are now a **top-20 holder of U.S. Treasuries (~$150B)** — Tether $127B, Circle $25B — ahead of Saudi Arabia and Germany (p.20). Supply reached **~$340B**, dominated by Tether (~$220B) and USDC; **99.8% of supply is USD-denominated** (p.21, p.23). Citi is cited projecting **$1.6-$3.7T by 2030** (p.23).

## DeFi and onchain app categories

The "world is coming onchain" section surveys app categories (p.24-31): **DEX** share of crypto spot trading rose from ~0% (2019) to a ~22% peak, ~19% currently (p.25); **perpetual futures** volume reached ~$1.15T with **Hyperliquid the leading perps venue by revenue (>$1B annualized)** (p.26); **RWAs ~$30B onchain**, led by private credit then U.S. Treasury debt, with **BlackRock the top issuer** (p.27); **DePIN** (Helium: 111,906 active hotspots, 1.41M daily users) (p.28); **>13M memecoins launched in a year** (Pump.fun dominant), framed as "a symptom of crypto's lack of regulated market frameworks" (p.29); NFTs shifting "from speculating to collecting" (p.30); and prediction markets (Polymarket, Kalshi) spiking to ~$1.9B at the U.S. election (p.31). Per-chain breakdowns for RWAs, stablecoins, and DEX activity are in the chains-and-infrastructure companion.

## U.S. regulation and policy

The report calls the U.S. environment the strongest ever for crypto (p.46-49). The **GENIUS Act** (stablecoin regime) passed bipartisanly (Senate 50R/18D, House 206R/102D) and was **signed into law** (p.47). The **CLARITY Act** (digital-asset market structure) passed the House (216R/78D) and is **progressing to the Senate, not yet signed** (p.47). Federal-agency actions (p.48): the DOJ Blanche memo and its statement that "merely writing code, without ill-intent, is not a crime"; the SEC dismissing/settling cases against Binance, Coinbase, Consensys, Gemini, Kraken, OpenSea, Robinhood, and Uniswap Labs, and launching a Crypto Task Force / "Project Crypto"; Treasury reversing the OFAC Tornado Cash designation; and Fed/FDIC/OCC rescinding restrictive crypto requirements. The token-revenue loop closes the section: users paid **$33B in fees → $18B revenue to projects → $4B accruing to tokens** (DefiLlama, annualized) (p.49).

## Nine forward predictions

The report's closing predictions (p.53): (1) market-structure legislation becomes a top policy priority; (2) stablecoin adoption accelerates, strengthening the USD; (3) crypto×AI addresses internet-scale challenges; (4) TradFi/fintechs double down; (5) throughput approaches the largest internet services; (6) a new RWA wave comes onchain; (7) more talent flows in; (8) more tokens generate revenue via fee switches; (9) new consumer products onboard the next user wave.
